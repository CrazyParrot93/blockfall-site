Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Blockfall is a puzzle game. This policy covers the Blockfall app for iPhone and the web version of the game. In short: Blockfall does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Blockfall has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party software development kits. The game never sends information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To remember your choices while you play, the game keeps a few settings on your device: your best score, the selected theme, and whether sound, music and vibration are turned on. These never leave your device and are cleared when the app or browser tab is closed.

Website hosting

The web version and these pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The iPhone app does not use this website to play.

Children

Blockfall is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
