How to play

Move and rotate the falling pieces to fill complete rows. Full rows clear and score points, and every 10 rows the level goes up and pieces fall faster. The game ends when the stack reaches the top.

Tap the board
Rotate
Swipe left or right
Move one column per step
Drag down
Soft drop (keep holding to keep dropping)
Flick down
Hard drop
Flick up, or tap the Hold box
Hold the current piece for later

The buttons under the board do the same things, and holding Left, Right or Soft keeps repeating.

Scoring

Clearing 1, 2, 3 or 4 rows at once scores 100, 300, 500 or 800 points, multiplied by the level. Soft drop earns 1 point per row and hard drop 2 points per row.

Common questions

I can't hear any sound

Check that the ring/silent switch on your iPhone is not set to silent, that the speaker button in the top bar is not muted, and that the volume is up. Background music is off by default: turn it on with the music-note button.

Can I turn off vibration?

Yes. Pause the game and tap Vibration.

My best score disappeared

Best scores are kept for the current session only and reset when the app is closed.

How do I switch themes?

Tap the half-circle button in the top bar to switch between Neon and Retro LCD.
